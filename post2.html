Love's Many Faces

Love isn't just wanting to dance in the rain.
It's wanting to curl up in the blanket together so you both don't catch a cold.
It's sweating at the proximity but not relinquishing the hold.

Love isn't just sharing clothes and a hairbrush.
It's sharing bits of your childhood and bites of your food.
It's sharing your joys and sorrows and mirroring each other's mood.

Love… isn't just sneaking out and splintering the rules.
It's jutting your chin out at the world if they're ever hurt.
It's laying in laps and braiding hair and twisting a nervous finger into their shirt.

Love isn't just an emotion.
It's Mumma’s way of life and the chink in my armour.
It's my fachhe sharing their poems before they confess to their paramour.

Love is not just beautiful.
It's a siren from the mists with a terrifying reputation;
It's a lonely witch in the forest who is hunted only for her vocation.

Love isn't just whimsical.
It's holding hands together and letting someone have your back,
When your Earth undergoes an axial tilt and this is all that keeps you on track.

Love… isn't just romantic.
Love is in pickle jars and toy cars and glow-in-the-dark stars.
Love is listening happily to bak-bak, uncaring about the hours.

Love isn't just this poem.
It's about whatever else you think of when you finish reading this.
It's everything you don't find here and wish you didn't have to miss.
