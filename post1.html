Freesia

Frantic, frenetic drum beats of the rain
Upon petals of wild freesias, withstanding in vain,
Against the call of being one with the air,
Diffusing fragrance isn't enough, they must not question the ‘where’
And ‘how’ of the road not taken,
Each season, uprooted and shaken
Til each inhale carries a bit of their essence;
With each gale, their identity lessens.

I'm a freesia too, my petals laden with dew,
Too burdened to fly but rooted firm and true.
I long to effuse myself into the world’s sinews,
Bits and pieces pulled apart, they can pick and choose
What they'd like to discard and what they'd like to consume,
Their palettes as impermanent as the filtered smoke into which they exhume,
They keep sullying their lungs and thanklessly demand
More, more and more until I simply can't-

I've run out. Or been stomped on.
Wrinkled and dried up, perhaps even chomped on.
Wishing for freedom secretly, wanting the wind under my wing,
Like a shooting star wishing to become a memorable thing.

Must I detonate in order to be remembered?
Can I not do much with a fleeting moment or a stray word?
I'm a freesia without freedom to choose how I fade,
Hell, even fading away might be a little above my paygrade.

It takes courage to make an impact on people, they say.
It takes much more to even face them, if you look at it my way.
Looking at them dead in the eye as they entitle themselves to your time,
Wishing for release - a fallacy; wanting to refuse - an absolute crime.

I'm freer than the creepers who can't rise without a tether,
But more caged than birds that challenge the skies despite the weather.
I'm stuck between deciding how much I can get away with while just a flower,
A freesia with foreign fantasies, rooted to its ivory tower.
